### S.T.A.R. 
Modular dynamic screenspace inventory built in Unity. Systems and ships have inventories. Those inventories can hold custom objects with various properties, A factory can build a weapon through it's interface, the weapon can be placed on the ship, that weapon can then be used to attack enemie ships through the weapons interface.

### Pastel
Radial UI. Render textures display selectable locations around the map. Shapes are all data driven, allowing it to adapt to any number of elements. Mask generated through a compute shader Colab with Eduardo Esquidero

### Resident Evil Style Inventory
Grid based inventory for objects of various shapes. Items seamlesly transition between game and inventory space
